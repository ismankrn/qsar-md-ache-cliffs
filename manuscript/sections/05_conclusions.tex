\section{Conclusions}

Across five descriptor sets of increasing physical detail, scored on three framings of
145 activity-cliff pairs of human acetylcholinesterase against a detection limit of
about $0.3$ in the coefficient of determination that was fixed before any of them was
scored, no structure-based representation was resolvably better than a two-dimensional
fingerprint on any framing. The one comparison the design did resolve runs against the
molecular dynamics descriptors on per-ligand potency, and the variance the ladder
leaves unexplained is far larger than assay reproducibility can account for, which
places the limitation in the descriptors rather than in the data or in the length of
the trajectories. A modest advantage remains possible and this design cannot see one,
so what would settle the question is the number of pairs and of targets rather than the
nanoseconds spent on each.
