\NOTE{Engage with \citet{husby2015} rather than citing its abstract. It reports
  that structure-based methods predict activity cliffs accurately, and the number
  behind that is template docking at a median 90\% using the X-ray pose of the
  \emph{stronger} ligand, plus a pocket ensemble selected with the cliff labels
  that the authors themselves call purely retrospective. The prospective arm,
  cross-docking, sits at a median 53\%. Both partners have crystal structures and
  no ligand-based baseline is run. That is the gap this study occupies: prospective,
  one receptor, a signed-difference endpoint, and a matched 2D baseline.}

  to cliffs with a stated detection limit, not the descriptor machinery.}
