Section~\ref{sec:rungs} gives the feature count of each rung but does not name the
descriptors, and the tables here do. Each is named as it appears in the deposited
descriptor tables, so a quantity discussed in the text can be traced to the column it
was computed in. Rungs~2, 2e and~4 share the panel of fourteen gorge-lining residues
and compute a per-residue quantity for each, which is why all three tables end with a
block of fourteen entries. The three differ in what that quantity is, namely a contact
count on one pose, the same count on eight, and the fraction of simulated frames in
which the contact was present. The last column of Table~\ref{tab:sirung4} is the
only place the reliability filter is reported descriptor by descriptor, as
Section~\ref{sec:rungs} gives no more than the number that survived it.
