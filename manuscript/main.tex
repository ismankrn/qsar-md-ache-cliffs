\subsection*{Data and code availability}

All analysis code is in a public repository at
\url{https://github.com/ismankrn/qsar-md-ache-cliffs}, archived at
\url{https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23498751}. The deposit carries the 229
per-run descriptor files, one JSON per trajectory and \SI{1.8}{\mega\byte} in
total, together with the curated ligand set, the frozen splits and every
derived table the figures and the reported numbers are computed from. Those
files are sufficient to reproduce every result in this paper without rerunning
a simulation.

The trajectories themselves are not deposited. Reduced protein and ligand
coordinates at \SI{100}{\pico\second}, \SI{2.6}{\giga\byte} over the 229 runs,
are available from the corresponding author on request. Any trajectory supplied
this way needs the treatment of the periodic boundary described in
Section~\ref{sec:md} before anything is measured on it, since the archives are
wrapped molecule-wise and the capped receptor is three separate chains. Over
the 229 archives an RMSD computed directly on one returns a median of
\SI{45}{\angstrom} for the receptor backbone and \SI{24}{\angstrom} for the
ligand, against the \SI{1.4}{\angstrom} and \SI{3.0}{\angstrom} the same
quantities give once the boundary has been handled.
